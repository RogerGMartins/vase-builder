Drainage guide

Use a free-draining container potting mix. Keep outlets clear, support each module on a level draining surface, and use pot feet or supporting battens if the surface blocks runoff. Printing, fit and what has been checked

Print the economy test plate first.

The five original connector tests share one plate. With nesting trays, the male and female ridge samples share that same plate. These check the structural mating profiles; inspect the close-fitting exterior seam on the first pair of bodies. The reduced fixtures preserve the mating profiles but save about 69% of solid test geometry compared with V3. They check clearance and bridge quality, not full-size strength. Both short and production wall keys print flat with build-plate supports, so the test checks the same print orientation.

Use the assembly guide above.

It lists quantities for your selected options and explains every component. The part inspector and underside view help identify each piece. Temporary brace membranes are cut away before assembly; they are not soil partitions.

Continuous built-in patterns.

Built-in relief wraps without the former smooth module borders. A matched module repeat keeps the phase aligned across interchangeable ends and straight modules, including rotated and scaled patterns. Honeycomb, mosaic and ripple repeat boundaries have also been corrected. Regular motif spacing adapts slightly so a whole number of repeats fits each module. The new close-fitting joint uses a 238.4 mm pitch; legacy joins use 240 mm. A smooth rim transition at the very top and bottom keeps the print contact and rim tidy. The default join removes the raised strip. A nominal 0.4 mm exterior assembly seam remains; the internal sockets retain 2 mm working space. Use one join style throughout a planter; the new and legacy bodies are different.

Texture images become geometry.

Upload a local PNG, JPEG or WebP, up to 10 MB and 16 megapixels. The tool creates a 128 × 128 grayscale height map, then samples it on the exterior mesh. White is raised and black is low; transparent pixels are low. Angle, scale, stretch, contrast up to 4×, inversion, offsets and tile size apply to real exported vertices. Boost image tonal range expands low-contrast maps. Imported relief uses 0.6–2.8 mm depth and a measured-distance wrap around the shaped end; displacement is applied after shaping to reduce stretch. Arbitrary images may still show module seams. Save design includes the parsed height map. Smooth or seamless images work best: arbitrary photos can create a visible repeat. Fine image detail below the mesh spacing will not be reproduced.

Shape choices.

End modules can be capsule, rounded rectangular, rectangular or faceted. Straight middle modules remain repeatable. Chamfered and soft lower edges retain a flat printable underside. Flare and waist shaping blend between straight joining bands; those bands retain their structural geometry, to preserve floor rails, keys and liners. Each overall shape has its own controls. The new bottom finish continues through joining posts and seam covers; the former wide inset that stopped before each joint has been replaced. Rounded blend is a printable eased curve, not an exact circular fillet. The outside top chamfer leaves functional socket and key seating geometry in place.

Your printer dimensions.

Set X, Y and Z under Print space. The tool repacks and rotates parts on their plates, checks height and includes your brim allowance. You can reserve a front-right corner; the packer conservatively leaves that whole X strip empty. Supported design range: 180–400 mm width and 140–400 mm height. A larger print space does not remove the design limits.

Drainage and PETG.

Use dry PETG and your proven filament profile, 8 walls, 100% infill for bodies/rails as the conservative default and conservative acceleration. Start near 0.24 mm layers. Slow tall walls above about 180 mm. Floor-slot roofs bridge approximately 18 × width / 240 mm; inspect the matching coupon for sagging. Do not generate supports inside those slots. Choose main-drain count, shape, size, distribution and outlet-channel depth; use small mesh patches where needed and keep outlets clear. Support each module on a level draining surface.

Export and tolerances.

3MF files are unsliced multi-plate projects. Open as a project in ElegooSlicer; choose your exact printer, nozzle and PETG settings before slicing. The selected envelope, wall count, layer height, solid-layer counts and infill are embedded. Connector objects override body infill to remain solid. Applying a different process preset may replace these values; review them after changing profiles. The selected envelope is embedded, but the tool is not a substitute for a printer profile or a check of purge/obstacle locations. Projects above 36 plates are blocked. Three favourites supplies three body styles and one shared connector kit. Legacy covered joins additionally include matching covered keys for each style.
